## Five-discipline assessment

**Correctness:** The inspected policy source shows a static queue-only decision boundary, not a proven end-to-end food-safety system. [backend/tests/test_decisions.py:18-24](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Menumind/blob/542fe2c9eccf7ad5093a62ba13be1306f8152521/backend/tests/test_decisions.py#L18-L24) checks unknown-choice coercion, but no reviewed evidence proves that real menu inputs, real model outputs and any real publication path compose safely.

**Automation/testing:** The backend has pytest coverage, including a [budget rejection case](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Menumind/blob/542fe2c9eccf7ad5093a62ba13be1306f8152521/backend/tests/test_budgets.py#L8-L19). Those tests were not run. The inspected frontend manifest lacks a test command, so no observed browser or accessibility gate protects the synthetic/live boundary in the operator UI.

**DevOps/operations:** `backend/evals/receipts.sqlite` appears in the tracked tree; contents and sensitivity were not inspected. `jobs`, limiter state and kill state are process-local in the sampled backend path. Restore, restart, multi-worker behavior, alerting, retention and rollback remain unverified.

**OWASP/security:** The repo’s [threat model:7-25](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/Menumind/blob/542fe2c9eccf7ad5093a62ba13be1306f8152521/specs/security/owasp-threat-model.md#L7-L25) is a plan, not proof. The sampled backend does show a configurable token check and a URL-fetch rejection step, but authz on receipts/jobs, upload handling, prompt-injection containment, XSS, dependency integrity, redaction and deployed CSP behavior were not independently validated here.

**UI/UX/accessibility:** Operators must be able to tell fixture/demo state from observed backend outcomes and understand that the inspected policy keeps publication off. Review keyboard navigation, focus, status announcements, contrast and error recovery with a real browser; none of that ran in this pass.

## Production blockers and owner questions

Who is the qualified food-safety owner, and who owns publication rights for menu data? Is any real publisher integration present and authorized, or is this revision intentionally queue-only? Who approves model/provider usage, restore drills, retention, incident handling and operator overrides? Do not use this prototype to guarantee a dish is safe, to promise autonomous publication, or to assert regulatory compliance.
